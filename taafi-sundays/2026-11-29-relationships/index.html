Taafi Sunday · November

Relationships got hard… or did we make them hard?

An honest evening about love, friendship and family, and the patterns we carry into every relationship.

WhenSun 29 Nov · 6:00–7:30 PM
WhereDubai (venue soon) or online
LanguageEnglish, Arabic when needed
PriceFrom AED 60
About this evening

How you show up in relationships: what you expect, what you fear, and which old patterns still make choices for you. Have relationships really become harder, or have we become so protective that even the people we love can’t reach us?

This is for you if…

  • You keep ending up in the same kind of relationship with different people.
  • You give a lot, and still feel unseen.
  • Asking for what you need brings guilt.
  • Conflict frightens you, or drains you.
  • You want closeness, but something keeps pulling you back.

What we’ll explore

  • 01Where our patterns come fromHow early experiences shape the way we love.
  • 02Expectations vs. needsAnd the stories we tell ourselves about others.
  • 03Boundaries that protectA relationship, instead of ending it.
  • 04A calmer nervous systemAnd how it changes the way you respond.

The evening

  1. 6:00Arrival and grounding
  2. 6:10The conversation: are we complicating it?
  3. 6:30Guided reflection on your relationship pattern
  4. 6:50Sharing circle, only if you want to
  5. 7:15Closing practice, and one thing to take home

You’ll leave with

  • A clearer picture of your own pattern.
  • One honest conversation you feel ready to have.
  • A simple grounding practice for moments of conflict.
  • The relief of knowing you’re not the only one.
Hosted by Alaa EldakrouriFormer lawyer of ten years, now an inner transformation mentor. Her evenings feel more like a conversation with someone who has been there than a lecture.
