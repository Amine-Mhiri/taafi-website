Questions

Good to know

Do I have to share?

No. Listening is enough. You only share if you want to.

Is it a therapy group?

No. It is a reflective group workshop. You are never asked for the details of your story, and you can step out, stay quiet or just listen at any time.

How does joining online work?

Choose the Online ticket. The live link is emailed before the evening, and you can keep your camera on or off.

Where is the venue?

In Dubai, UAE. The venue details are emailed to everyone registered before the evening.

What should I bring?

A notebook, a pen, and yourself, as you are.

Can I get a refund or transfer my ticket?

Tickets aren’t refundable, but you can transfer your seat to someone else up to 48 hours before the evening. If an evening is cancelled, you get a full refund.
