Questions

Before you join

Who is the Circle for?

Anyone who wants steady support between the bigger moments: clients, Sunday regulars, or someone just starting. Mostly women; everyone respectful is welcome.

Where does it happen?

In a private WhatsApp community, so it lives where you already are. You can mute it anytime. Live workshops are on a video link.

Is it therapy?

No. It is a supportive community for reflection. For something heavy, a 1:1 session or a professional is the right place.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, in two clicks. You keep access until the end of the month you’ve paid for.
