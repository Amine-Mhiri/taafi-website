My story

You don’t need to almost die to start truly living.

These are the moments that shaped who I am and why I do this work. I share them not for sympathy, but because somewhere in them you might find a piece of your own story.

“The greatest investment you can ever make is the one within.”

Who I am, who I work with, and what a first session feels like. In my own voice, in English and Egyptian Arabic.

Three turning points

01My body, 2017–2022

The illness no one could explain

Something in my body went quiet. Doctor after doctor, test after test, and no answer that made sense. Then in 2022 my life broke open, personally and professionally, at the same time.

A therapist finally said the sentence that changed everything: “I don’t think your problem is physical.” I began to listen to my body instead of silencing it, and slowly it found its rhythm again. That listening became my path into holistic work.

“My body started speaking the language my voice never had the courage to use.”
02 · Bali, September 2024

A near-death experience, and three lights.

A day after I flew to Bali to breathe, I had an accident. I saw my life replay, then three lights, each with a message. Since that day, there is no place in my heart for fear.

LightLet go of every attachment to what you own.
FreedomOnce you let go, you arrive at freedom.
HappinessAnd after freedom, a happiness that stays.

The three phases of the Gratitude Journal come from these three lights. See the journal

03Where I come from

The girl who was never asked what she wanted

I grew up where a girl is quietly treated differently from her brother. He had a say. I learned early that I didn’t. Something in me refused that, so I built myself, by myself, and I always felt held from somewhere bigger than me.

Today I help women heal their sense of worth and see themselves again, not through the picture they were handed growing up.

“You were never required to earn the right to exist exactly as you are.”
Before this work

Ten years in law. Then burnout.

I spent over a decade as a legal counsel in high-pressure, deadline-driven rooms, sitting inside people’s hardest battles and asking what was really driving the fight, the fear, the refusal to let go.

I know what it is to live for work and wear burnout like a badge. That is exactly why I understand your world.

My approach

The mind, the heart, and the soul.

Three voices guide every decision you make. None of them is wrong. Healing is learning which one is speaking, and bringing them back into balance.

The mindProtects youIt remembers past pain and chooses safety, sometimes over a fuller life.
The heartLets you liveIt takes the risk, feels the joy and the pain, and sees you as whole.
The soulServes something biggerIt points to meaning and purpose, even when that isn’t the easy way.
