What a session is, and isn’t.

So you arrive knowing exactly what you are saying yes to.

It is

  • A private, confidential hour with Alaa, online.
  • Conversation, breathwork and body-based practice, at your pace.
  • Respectful of your culture, your family and your beliefs.
  • Practical: you always leave with something to use.

It isn’t

  • Medical or psychiatric treatment, or a diagnosis.
  • Crisis care. If you are in danger, contact local emergency services first.
  • A quick fix, or a promise of a particular outcome.
  • Religious instruction. Spiritually aware, never tied to one belief.

Questions

Before you book

Can I start with one session?

Yes. Many people begin with one to see if the work feels right, then continue session by session or choose the package. There’s no pressure.

What happens in a session?

It depends on where you are that day. We might talk through what’s weighing on you, notice a pattern, use breath or body practice to release tension, and shape one tool for your week.

Is this religious?

It is spiritually aware but not tied to any religion, and every belief is respected. Many clients find the practices sit comfortably alongside their own faith; others bring none. Both are welcome.

I work in a high-pressure job. Will you get my world?

Alaa spent over ten years as a legal counsel in deadline-driven environments. She knows what it is to live for work and ignore your body.

I’m dealing with family and cultural expectations.

Healing doesn’t mean abandoning your culture. It means finding your own voice within it, with respect for where you come from.

I’ve tried therapy before. Why would this be different?

Talk is part of it, but we also work with the body and nervous system, where stress is often held. Whether it suits you is something only you can feel, which is why one session is a good start.

Are sessions online or in person?

Online, on a private video link, so you can join from anywhere.
