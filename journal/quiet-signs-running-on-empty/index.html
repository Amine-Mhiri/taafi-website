Burnout · 4 min read · by Alaa Eldakrouri

The quiet signs you’re running on empty.

Burnout rarely arrives as a collapse. More often it builds quietly, over years of pushing through, of being the reliable one, of telling yourself it’s just a busy season. I spent ten years in law believing exactly that.

Here are five signs we’ve learned to call normal.

  1. Unfinished thoughtsYou start a sentence in your head and lose it. Your mind is so full that nothing gets to land.
  2. Rest that doesn’t restoreYou sleep, take the weekend, even travel, and wake up just as tired. Your body rested; your nervous system never switched off.
  3. Reading everyone’s feelings but your ownYou know instantly when someone in the room is upset. Ask how you feel, and there’s a blank.
  4. Shorter patienceSmall things land heavily. You snap, then feel guilty, then push harder.
  5. “Later” keeps movingYour own needs live in a future that never arrives: after this project, after this month, after everyone else is fine.

If two or more of these sound familiar, that is information, not failure.

You don’t need to change your whole life this week. Start with one honest conversation: with yourself on paper, with someone you trust, or with me.

This article is for reflection and is not medical advice. If you are struggling, please speak to a doctor or a mental-health professional.
